Bearing Blade — Privacy Policy

Effective July 2026

The short version

Bearing Blade collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no server that receives your data. Everything you record stays on your device.

Data storage

Your readings, photos, settings, and calibration records are stored locally on your device (browser IndexedDB and localStorage). They are never transmitted to us. Deleting the app or clearing site data removes them permanently — export regularly.

Device sensors

The app uses your device's magnetometer, accelerometer, and GPS to take geological measurements. Sensor and location data are processed on-device only and attached to readings you choose to save. Location is requested only when you start the compass.

Network requests

When online, the app may request: map tiles from OpenStreetMap and Macrostrat; magnetic declination from NOAA (sends only coordinates, no identifiers); and geologic-unit lookups from Macrostrat (coordinates only). Each service receives your IP address as part of any web request, governed by their own policies. All features degrade gracefully offline.

Your exports

CSV, GeoJSON, and KML exports are generated on-device and saved where you choose. Sharing them is entirely under your control.
